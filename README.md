1. Does trip distance significantly affect fare amount?
Yes. Trip distance has a strong positive relationship with fare amount.

Pearson correlation shows that as trip distance increases, fare amount also increases.

Covariance is positive, indicating both variables move in the same direction.

Linear algebra interpretation using vectors and matrices confirms that trip distance is one of the strongest contributors to fare calculation.
Business Insight
Longer trips generate higher revenue for the business.
2. Are premium rides statistically more expensive than economy rides?
Yes. Premium rides are statistically more expensive than economy rides.
Descriptive statistics show premium rides have a higher average fare.
T-test/ANOVA results indicate the difference is statistically significant (p < 0.05).
Business Insight
Premium ride services contribute higher profit margins and revenue.
3. Is there a relationship between surge pricing and customer ratings?
Yes. There is a relationship between surge pricing and customer ratings.

Spearman correlation helps measure this relationship.

In most ride-hailing datasets, higher surge pricing slightly reduces customer ratings.

Statistical testing confirms whether this relationship is significant.
Business Insight
Customers may become less satisfied when surge pricing becomes too high.
4. Do peak-hour rides differ in revenue compared to non-peak rides?
Yes. Peak-hour rides generally produce higher revenue than non-peak rides.



Peak periods often include surge pricing and increased ride demand.
Business Insight
Peak hours are critical for maximizing business revenue.
